# MANONO MANPHIS ENGINE AUDIT

This document serves as a critical technical and operational review of the Manono Manphis "Export OS" and "Advertising Engine."

---

## 1. CORE MODULES
**Status**: High-Fidelity / Trade-Focused

### **Dashboard (The Pulse)**
- **Role**: Real-time operational oversight.
- **Key Features**: 
    - `KpiCard` with interactive Sparklines for trend visualization.
    - Status-weighted badges for orders and payments.
    - Recent activity feed focused on order progression.
- **Critical Insight**: Currently optimized for physical volume (tons/liters). The engine is ready to receive a "Digital Ad Context" to track billboard slot occupancy alongside export revenue.

### **Analytics (The Intelligence)**
- **Role**: Deep data modeling and forecasting.
- **Key Features**:
    - **Revenue Forecast**: Composed charts (Area + Bar + Line) for predicting cash flow.
    - **Performance Radar**: Multi-dimensional radar chart for tracking business health metrics (Win Rate, Lead Quality, etc.).
    - **Market Map**: Scatter charts mapping country-level revenue against order volume.
    - **Team Leaderboard**: Sales agent tracking with micro-metrics (calls, emails, deals).
- **Critical Insight**: The Radar chart is a powerful tool for service-based businesses. It can be easily adapted to track "Billboard Uptime" and "Loop Efficiency."

### **Reports (The Archive)**
- **Role**: Document generation and scheduling.
- **Key Features**:
    - Automated scheduling engine for recurring business reviews.
    - Multi-format support (PDF for visual reports, CSV/Spreadsheet for data).
    - Archive tracking with storage management logic.
- **Critical Insight**: This is the "compliance" heart of the system. It will eventually handle the automated "Proof of Play" reports required by advertising clients.

---

## 2. SALES & CRM MODULES
**Status**: High-Fidelity / Integrated CRM

### **Leads & CRM (The Pipeline)**
- **Role**: Conversion tracking and lead qualification.
- **Key Features**:
    - **Visual Kanban**: Drag-and-drop board for stages (New, Contacted, Negotiating, Won).
    - **Segmentation**: Priority tagging (Hot, VIP, Bulk) for sales focus.
    - **Valuation**: Real-time pipeline value calculation based on potential deal sizes.
- **Critical Insight**: This is a robust engine. By adding a "Billboard" lead type, you can manage your advertising sales team through the exact same workflow as your commodity brokers.

### **Quotations (The Offer)**
- **Role**: Professional price proposals and negotiation tracking.
- **Key Features**:
    - **Lifecycle Tracking**: Monitor when quotes are "Viewed" by customers (crucial for follow-ups).
    - **Multi-Currency Support**: Handles global trade with diverse currencies (USD, EUR, GBP).
    - **Validity Management**: Automatic tracking of expiration dates for quotes.
- **Critical Insight**: This engine is ready to generate billboard rental contracts and service agreements, moving away from manual pricing.

### **Customers (The Network)**
- **Role**: Relationship management and buyer profiling.
- **Key Features**:
    - **Revenue Tiering**: Clients are segmented into VIP, Trusted, and New categories.
    - **Market Insights**: Tracks orders and revenue per country/market.
    - **Quick Actions**: Integrated communication triggers (One-click Call/Email).
- **Critical Insight**: The customer database needs a "Service Interest" tag to distinguish between someone buying Shea Butter and someone renting a Billboard in Accra.

### **Communications (The Inbox)**
- **Role**: Unified omnichannel conversation center.
- **Key Features**:
    - **Channel Integration**: Combines Email, Phone, WhatsApp, and Internal Notes into one thread.
    - **Response Tracking**: Monitors "Average Response Time" for team accountability.
    - **Contextual History**: Every message is linked directly to a customer or lead record.
- **Critical Insight**: This is the "Voice" of the engine. It ensures no advertising inquiry or export request falls through the cracks.

---

## 3. OPERATIONS MODULES
**Status**: High-Density / Supply Chain Ready

### **Order Management (The Lifecycle)**
- **Role**: End-to-end tracking of customer purchases.
- **Key Features**:
    - **Progress Tracking**: Visual progress bars showing the fulfillment percentage.
    - **Shipping Context**: Real-time integration of shipping method (Air/Sea) and ETAs.
    - **Financial Sync**: Links payment status (Paid/Pending) directly to order fulfillment.
- **Critical Insight**: An "Order" for a billboard is a Campaign. The "Shipping" logic can be repurposed to track the "Go-Live" date of the digital ad on the physical board.

### **Inventory (The Stock)**
- **Role**: Warehouse and stock level oversight.
- **Key Features**:
    - **Stock Partitioning**: Separates "Total Stock" from "Reserved" (already sold) and "Available."
    - **Low Stock Alerts**: Automated warnings when raw materials (Honey/Cashew) hit critical levels.
    - **Grade Tracking**: Categorizes products by quality (Premium vs. Standard).
- **Critical Insight**: For billboards, "Stock" is time. We can use this engine to track "Available Minutes/Slots" per month, ensuring you never over-sell a billboard's capacity.

### **Logistics & Shipping (The Pipeline)**
- **Role**: Global movement and documentation tracking.
- **Key Features**:
    - **Step-by-Step Timeline**: Tracks order confirmed -> production -> transit -> delivery.
    - **Document Vault**: Quick access to Bill of Lading, Invoices, and Certificates of Origin.
    - **Metric focus**: Tracks "Average Transit Time" to optimize supply chain speed.
- **Critical Insight**: This is the "Hard" side of the business. While billboards don't move, the **Document Vault** feature is perfect for storing the legal permits and site photos for your billboard locations.

### **Production (The Processing)**
- **Role**: Sourcing and quality control tracking.
- **Key Features**:
    - **Batch Management**: Tracks raw agricultural products through processing and packaging.
    - **Quality Gatekeeping**: Dedicated "Quality Check" stage to ensure export standards.
    - **Facility Oversight**: Monitors which facility and supervisor are handling which batch.
- **Critical Insight**: "Production" for billboards is maintenance. You can use this to track when a screen was last serviced, who the supervisor was, and if the "Quality Check" (brightness/pixels) was passed.

---

## 4. FINANCE MODULES
**Status**: Transactional / Multi-Currency

### **Payments (The Cashflow)**
- **Role**: Collection tracking and channel analysis.
- **Key Features**:
    - **Method Distribution**: Pie charts tracking revenue by channel (Bank, Card, Mobile Money).
    - **Aging Tracking**: Identifies "Overdue" payments for debt collection.
    - **Installment Support**: Designed to handle partial payments and balances.
- **Critical Insight**: This engine is ready to handle recurring monthly billboard rentals and can be integrated with Paystack/Flutterwave for automated ad-slot renewals.

### **Invoices (The Billing)**
- **Role**: Formal financial documentation.
- **Key Features**:
    - **Order Linking**: Every invoice is tied back to a specific order/deal ID.
    - **Flexible Terms**: Support for "Net 30" or "Upfront" payment structures.
    - **PDF Generation**: Integrated engine for producing professional download-ready invoices.
- **Critical Insight**: The invoice engine can be customized to include billboard-specific tax items and creative design fees alongside standard commodity costs.

---

## 5. COMPLIANCE & TRADE MANAGEMENT
**Status**: Regulatory / Global-Ready

### **Documents (The Vault)**
- **Role**: Secure storage and expiry tracking for certificates/licenses.
- **Key Features**:
    - **Proactive Alerts**: Status tracking for "Expiring Soon" vs "Expired" documents.
    - **Categorization**: Groups documents into Export Licenses, Contracts, Quality Certs, etc.
    - **Global Mapping**: Links specific compliance documents to their respective countries.
- **Critical Insight**: This "Vault" is where you will store your billboard land-use permits and structural integrity certificates, with automated reminders for renewal.

### **Countries & Regions (The Market)**
- **Role**: Strategic market management and trade restrictions.
- **Key Features**:
    - **Market Health**: Tracks revenue and buyer density per country.
    - **Trade Barriers**: Monitors "Import Duties" and "Restricted" status for specific zones.
    - **Operational Data**: Stores "Lead Times" and "Shipping Zones" for each market.
- **Critical Insight**: While built for international exports, this can be adapted to manage local "Regions" (e.g., Accra Central vs. Kumasi) to track advertising performance by city.

### **Shipping Rules (The Freight Engine)**
- **Role**: Zone-based pricing and freight calculations.
- **Key Features**:
    - **Weight-Based Logic**: Calculates rates based on kg ranges and transit days.
    - **Active/Inactive Toggles**: Allows for quick adjustment of shipping rates as fuel/freight costs change.
    - **Method Icons**: Distinct logic for Sea, Air, Express, and Road transport.
- **Critical Insight**: This "Rule Engine" is a blueprint for billboard pricing. Instead of kg, we can set rules based on "Duration" or "Location Tier" to calculate billboard rental costs automatically.

## 6. ADMINISTRATION MODULES
**Status**: Secure / Enterprise-Grade

### **Users & Roles (The Access)**
- **Role**: Identity and access management (IAM).
- **Key Features**:
    - **Granular Roles**: Pre-defined roles for Admin, Sales, Logistics, Finance, and Compliance.
    - **Permissions Matrix**: Detailed control over who can create, edit, or delete data in each module.
    - **User Lifecycle**: Management of active users, pending invitations, and suspensions.
- **Critical Insight**: This allows you to have a "Billboard Manager" who has full access to the advertising engine but cannot see your confidential cashew export prices.

### **Activity Logs (The Audit)**
- **Role**: Transparency and security monitoring.
- **Key Features**:
    - **Complete Trail**: Every action (Login, Create, Update, Delete, Export) is logged with a timestamp.
    - **User Attribution**: Shows exactly who performed an action and from which IP address.
    - **Security Oversight**: Identifies failed login attempts and unauthorized data exports.
- **Critical Insight**: This ensures accountability. If a billboard rental rate is changed, you will know exactly who did it and when.

### **Settings (The Configuration)**
- **Role**: Global system-wide defaults.
- **Key Features**:
    - **Financial Profile**: Management of Tax IDs, Legal Names, and headquarters data.
    - **System Behavior**: FX Rate sources, default currencies, and tax-inclusive pricing toggles.
    - **Gateway Control**: Toggle-based management of Stripe, SWIFT, and other payment channels.
- **Critical Insight**: This is where you configure the "Legal Personality" of your business for all the documents generated by the engine.

---

## 7. SYSTEM MODULES
**Status**: Proactive / Automated

### **Notifications (The Alert Center)**
- **Role**: Actionable system-wide messaging.
- **Key Features**:
    - **Priority Management**: "High Priority" alerts for critical issues like expired licenses or overdue payments.
    - **Category Filtering**: Users can filter notifications by business area (Leads, Shipments, Compliance).
    - **Real-time Updates**: Pulse-animations for unread alerts to ensure nothing is missed.
- **Critical Insight**: This is where your team will receive alerts when an advertiser's "Loop Cycle" is ending on a billboard.

### **Automation (The Engine)**
- **Role**: No-code workflow orchestration.
- **Key Features**:
    - **Trigger-Action Logic**: "When [X] happens, do [Y]" rules for eliminating manual tasks.
    - **Workflow Visualizer**: A clear map of the Quote-to-Cash automation flow.
    - **Efficiency Metrics**: Tracks "Hours Saved" and "Success Rate" for automated processes.
- **Critical Insight**: This is the most powerful part of the engine. We can automate the entire billboard booking process: *When Payment is Verified -> Automatically Notify Billboard Logistics -> Update Website Availability.*

---

**FINAL AUDIT CONCLUSION**:
The Manono Manphis Engine is a world-class, production-ready platform. It is currently a "Ferrari" configured for Agricultural Export. By injecting the Digital Billboard Service into these existing modules—using the **Automation** for bookings, the **Finance** engine for rentals, and the **Logistics** logic for site maintenance—you will have a unified business engine that is unparalleled in efficiency.
